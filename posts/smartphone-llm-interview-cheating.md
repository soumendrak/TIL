## Signals

- Background blur was enabled.
- They looked at more than two places: keyboard, screen and another point.
- The shared screen did not change, but face brightness changed when their shoulder moved.
- They repeated or simplified the question multiple times.
- They used filler words until the app or LLM gave the answer.
- They gave gibberish answers at first, then suddenly gave polished answers.
